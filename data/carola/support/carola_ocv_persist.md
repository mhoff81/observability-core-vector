# carola_ocv_persist.py — at which spatial scale does deck persistence appear?

*Carolabrücke deck, 436 dates of the 69x125 support chip cut around the deck anchor, split at the 2024-09-11 collapse.*

Schema `ocv-persist-v1`. Generated by `code/carola/carola_ocv_persist.py`; `--verify` recomputes every number below from the committed outputs.

## The answer

- 0 of 1755 deck-band cells pass the year-round gates before the collapse, against 35 of 21420 ring cells.
- Over the whole 585-cell support the deck block has dom 0.03, RV 0.87, D_A 0.053; the ring null at the same cell count has dom 0.03, RV 0.93, D_A 0.052.
- The smallest D_A anywhere on the ladder is 0.057 at band_39x15 (585 cells); the ring null at the same N gives 0.063, i.e. the same number: the drop is the 1/sqrt(N) of incoherent averaging, not a scatterer.
- No ladder step, from one cell to the full 585-cell support, leaves its same-N ring null on the phase side (RV low, gamma high) after Holm, so persistence does not appear at any spatial scale.
- Blocks that are brighter than the null (dom high): DESCENDING/IW2 deck_3x3 (brightest 0.723 against 0.676 for the null extreme, p<0.001), DESCENDING/IW2 margin_left_39x2 (1 of 1 blocks, p 0.028) — brightness concentration, which is not phase stability.
- A planted scatterer of 8x the local cell mean reads as RV 0.75 in 11 cells but 0.99 in 585 (mean of the 3 group(s)): at fixed relative brightness, aggregating dilutes a scatterer instead of sharpening it.
- The band bins beside the carriageway are bright and incoherent (margin_left_39x2 in DESCENDING/IW2, 78 cells: dom 0.30 against a null of 0.17; RV 0.83-1.31 over the margin blocks). They sit outside the 39 m deck, so they cannot be the deck scatterer, and their phase is not stable either.

So: no scatterer on the 39 m deck is simultaneously bright (at least 8x the local block mean) and phase-stable, and aggregating the band does not create one. Where an aggregated block does look calmer than a single cell — around 15 to 45 cells — the ring null at the same cell count is calmer by exactly the same factor, because that is what averaging N independent speckle fields does: `D_A` falls like `1/sqrt(N)`. `RV`, which does not improve with N, stays at 1 (pure speckle) at every scale, and `dom` falls like `H_N/N`, the brightest of N speckle cells over their sum — both are printed next to the same-N ring null so the reader can check it cell by cell.

## The ladder, before the collapse

`mask` is the share of dates whose mask touches the block; `out` is the number of blocks that leave the same-N ring null on the persistence side (RV low or dom high). `null` columns are medians of the ring placements, `p05-p95` the 5th and 95th percentiles. Single cells and 3x3 blocks are swept over the whole deck (429 and 333 blocks), and are tested as one question — is the most extreme block outside the most extreme of as many ring placements — because 429 blocks cannot be Holm-tested against a finite null; every other step is tiled and Holm-tested block by block.

At a fixed relative brightness the block gets harder to see as it grows: 0.75 in 11 cells against 0.99 in 585, and in 6 of the 6 group-by-brightness comparisons in which the single-line block can see the scatterer at all (`RV` < 0.5) the whole support reads a higher `RV`. The middle of the ladder is not monotone, because `f` is measured against each block's own mean and a block that contains a brighter patch plants a brighter scatterer at the same `f`; where the finest block detects, the two ends never cross, so nothing here sees at 585 cells a scatterer that the finest block misses at the same relative brightness — which is why the ladder is reported at the scale that still resolves a cell.

## What the numbers do not say

- the chip is cut around the deck anchor per date, so a deck-fixed scatterer keeps its chip index while ground clutter does not; that is the right frame for a deck-fixed scatterer and the wrong frame for an absolute ground claim, and registration residual (about +-1-2 cells) is why the single-cell row is reported but is never the basis of the verdict.
- the ring null shares the chip's geometry but not its view geometry, so it bounds what incoherent averaging does at the same cell count, not what the deck must look like.
- gamma_g still carries unremoved atmosphere at 12-day repeat; it is reported and never used as the gate.
- the gates are ported from the year-round test exactly; they were not re-tuned for this ladder, which is why a zero on the band can be read against the ring.
- no label for the structure and no timestamp: the report carries numbers, dates and a verdict about scatterers only.
