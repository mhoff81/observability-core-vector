# KDLO OCV observability package — does the coherence contrast separate the epochs, or the calendar?

Figures A–E of `research/observability-core-vector/` for the KDLO-TV site. This file is generated by `code/kdlo/fig_kdlo_ocv_paper.py` and deliberately contains no wall-clock timestamp, so two runs produce identical bytes (the PNG figures are deterministic for the same reason).

## 1. Question and design

The **observability core vector** here is `OCV = [gamma2, P, D]` — the interferometric coherence `gamma^2` of the KDLO acquisition plus two dimensions of its **echo mask**: the persistence `P` (how often a mask pixel is masked across the 64 dates) and the density `D = A / bbox_area`. The full **6D echo-mask vector** of this package is `[gamma2, P, D, A, F, S]` (`A` = masked pixels = `coherence_masked_pixels`, `F` = number of 8-connected components, `S` = ||mask centroid - peak pixel|| in px). The contrast under test is not a damage label but an **epoch**:

- `pre` — pre-collapse (standing mast, 2022-06-02..2022-12-11): **16** acquisitions;
- `rebuild` — during the rebuild (2023-05-04..2024-09-19): **43** acquisitions;
- the single acquisition **2022-12-23** in between belongs to neither epoch and is in **no** state statistic of this package.

Because the two states are disjoint time windows, every effect size below is reported twice: once on all rows and once on the **month-matched** subset, and the classifier of panel C is run against a **weather-only** competitor. The design cannot prove causation; it can show which alternative explanations survive.

## 2. The record, and what was pinned

Input is the committed `kdlo/kdlo_ocv_channels.csv` (`60` acquisitions, sha256 `21dfe5b649c535ad...`), rebuilt from the two committed raw files (`kdlo/fig_kdlo_1_raw.json`, `kdlo/fig_kdlo_2_raw.json`) plus a one-time read-only extraction of nine Postgres columns (`kdlo/kdlo_db_extra.json`). Nothing is re-measured and no network is used.

The echo-mask dimensions are recomputed from the committed full-dwell strip cache `strips/` (64 strips, manifest sha256 `2c2e5fde8ba0c2a8...`) with the rule of the pipeline itself — so `A` reproduces `coherence_masked_pixels` exactly and the recomputed masked coherence reproduces `coherence_gamma2` to 1e-12 on all 60 acquisitions (the generator refuses to write the CSV otherwise). `P` is a property of the whole record (the 64 dates), so it cannot be checked against a single row.

Recomputed against the four committed reference JSONs: **2985 checks, 0 failures** (2985 exact, 0 within 1e-12; max |deviation| 0). Of those, 128 are structural checks of `kdlo_s1_availability.json` and 67 of `kdlo_fem_frequencies.json` — those two files are catalogue and eigenmode-solver output and are **not** rederivable from the record, so they are pinned structurally and reported honestly as such.

## 4. The epoch problem: is it the collapse, or the calendar?

`pre` holds 2022-06-02..2022-12-11 and `rebuild` 2023-05-04..2024-09-19, so the overlap of the two calendars is months 6..12: restricting both states to it drops the 15 `rebuild` acquisitions of Jan..May (the months `pre` cannot match) and leaves 16 vs. 28 acquisitions. The delta falls from +0.529 to **+0.429** — smaller, but far from gone.

## 5. The echo-mode split: geometry or damage?

The pipeline's own discriminator (verbatim from the project scripts) calls a return *compact* when `A <= 10 px` and *distributed* when `A >= 25 px`. Compact acquisitions go from **1** in `pre` to **12** in `rebuild`, and the median mask size drops from 120 px to 76 px. The distributed subset alone (n 14 vs. 30) still shows delta **+0.429** for `gamma^2` — i.e. the coherence change is not produced *only* by the new compact returns.

Median `gamma^2` by echo mode: compact 0.134509, distributed 0.004980. Compact echoes sit at *high* coherence, so they pull the `rebuild` median up: this is exactly the alternative reading — a change of **scattering geometry** rather than a change of **tower condition** — and the record cannot separate the two. The intermediate days (2022-11-17, 2024-04-16) belong to neither bucket and are excluded from both.

## 6. The 2-class classifier and its handicaps

Four models are fitted on the same 59 state acquisitions, all leave-one-out: `gamma2` alone (the baseline), the mask-morphology triple `[P, D, S]`, the OCV headline `[gamma2, P, D]` (shown here) and the full 6D echo-mask vector `[gamma2, P, D, A, F, S]`; panel D reports their out-of-fold scores. The table below is the headline model at lambda = 0.5.

Leave-one-out LDA on the 59 state acquisitions (chance balanced accuracy 0.50):

|  | pred `pre` | pred `rebuild` |
|---|---|---|
| true `pre` | 15 | 1 |
| true `rebuild` | 28 | 15 |

Accuracy 0.508, balanced accuracy **0.643**. The honest competitor — a classifier that sees only the acquisition weather (58 complete rows, `wind_speed_ms`, `wind_gust_ms`, `temperature_c`, `precipitation_mm`, `snow_depth_m`) — reaches at best 0.457, i.e. it does not beat chance. So the separation is not something the weather already carries.

Label permutation (1000 shuffles of the epoch label, full leave-one-out pipeline re-run each time): observed balanced accuracy 0.643 against a null mean of 0.496 and a 95th percentile of 0.620 — empirical p = **0.023**.

That p = **0.023** is below 0.05 and the observed value sits above the null's 95th percentile: with the echo-mask dimensions added, the per-acquisition classifier **does** separate the two epochs beyond chance. It must not be read as more than that — the labels are epochs (a year-long drift or a pipeline re-baseline would separate them just as well), and the 1D `gamma2` baseline alone scores **0.686**, i.e. *higher* than the OCV's 0.643: the mask dimensions change the permutation null, not the decision boundary. The contrast of Figure B is a difference of *distributions* either way.

Three handicaps remain, none of which the pinning can remove: the labels are epochs (so a year-long drift, a sensor re-baseline or a change of orbit behaviour would separate them just as well); the leave-one-out CV only says "a model fitted on the rest of the record scores this acquisition the same way", not "this acquisition was not disturbed"; and every number here comes from a single site with a single geometry (S1A descending, one relative orbit), so there is no second site against which to check.

## 7. What this package does *not* claim

- **Not a damage label.** `pre` and `rebuild` are epochs. Every effect size is an epoch contrast; nothing here says the mast was intact or damaged.
- **Not a causal statement.** A contrast on two disjoint time windows cannot distinguish "the tower changed" from "something else that changed with the tower". The month-matched column and the weather competitor of §4 are the only defences, and they are partial.
- **Not "after the reconstruction".** The record ends 2024-09-19 while the antenna reached 1,542 ft on 2024-08-19, so the second epoch is *during* the rebuild.
- **Not independent evidence.** `gamma^2` and `A` come from the same interferogram and the same pipeline; the phase channels come from the same processing chain. Coherence, mask size and phase activity are three views of one scattering geometry, not three experiments.
- **Not a second site.** The LUMO and Bautzen packages in this repository are separate sites with their own records; the agreement or disagreement between the three is a cross-site question this package does not answer.
- **Not a re-measurement.** The two reference files that are not rederivable (`kdlo_s1_availability.json`, `kdlo_fem_frequencies.json`) are pinned structurally (128 + 67 checks) and treated as given; only the two channel JSONs are recomputed field by field from the committed record.
- **Not a large sample.** 16 `pre` vs. 43 `rebuild` acquisitions on one geometry; strata with fewer than 3 acquisitions on one side are reported as `n/a` instead of being silently dropped, and the 1 missing repeat day(s) of part 1 are reported rather than interpolated.
